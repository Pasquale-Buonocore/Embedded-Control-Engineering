# Lesson M02 — Poles, Zeros, and Controller Design

## Goal

Given a transfer function and a PI/PID controller, predict how changing the gains moves the closed-loop poles and changes the response. Explain why a controller zero can simplify a nominal model, and demonstrate why exact pole-zero cancellation is fragile.

## Topics

Characteristic equation, root locus, Bode plots, and an RL plant with PI control. Compare nominal pole-zero cancellation with parameter error and delay.
