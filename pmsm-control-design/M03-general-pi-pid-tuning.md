# Lesson M03 — Tuning a PI/PID in General

## Goal

Tune a PI/PID from measurable requirements, then verify tracking, disturbance rejection, stability margin, and actuator effort. Choose and justify a tuning method when the plant model is known, approximate, or unavailable.

## Topics

Model-based pole placement, frequency-domain loop shaping using crossover and phase margin, and practical step-response tuning. Derivative filtering, setpoint weighting, anti-windup, and a repeatable tuning procedure.
