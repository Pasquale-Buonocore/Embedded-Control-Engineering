# Lesson M04 — PMSM Plant for Control

## Goal

Derive the electrical and mechanical plant seen by each of the current, speed, and position controllers. Identify which motor and load parameters determine their dynamics and which assumptions make a simple loop model valid.

## Topics

The d-q voltage equations, torque equation, and mechanical dynamics. Rs, Ld, Lq, flux linkage, inertia J, friction B, load torque, and the control-relevant differences between SPMSM and IPMSM.
