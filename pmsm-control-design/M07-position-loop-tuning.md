# Lesson M07 — Tuning the Position Loop

## Goal

Tune a position loop over the verified speed and current loops to follow a feasible motion profile within specified position error and overshoot. Identify when trajectory shaping or feedforward improves performance and when mechanics limit the achievable bandwidth.

## Topics

Bandwidth separation, position P/PD or two-degree-of-freedom structures, trajectory generation, velocity and acceleration feedforward, following error, resonances, and backlash.
