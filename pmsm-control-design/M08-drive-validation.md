# Lesson M08 — Validating the Complete Drive

## Goal

Produce an acceptance report showing that all three loops meet their targets across the intended operating range, including disturbances and saturation. Use failed tests to identify which loop or model assumption must be revisited.

## Topics

Reference and load steps, frequency response, saturation recovery, parameter changes, low- and high-speed operation, quantization, and noise. Record model assumptions, units, targets, and measured evidence; iterate from the innermost loop outward.
